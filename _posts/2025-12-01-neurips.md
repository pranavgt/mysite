So glad I could squeeze in NeurIPS'25 right before finals, a whirlwind that was dense in the best way possible. Talks, posters, hallway conversations, and a constant sense of standing right at the edge of what we understand about AI, neural networks, and the long arc toward AGI. It was exciting and honestly a bit overwhelming to see how many frontiers are open at once.

It was especially refreshing to see presentations from my own college, UPenn, alongside work from international universities. Absorbing research from so many different places made it clear how global and collaborative this space really is. While some narratives frame AI as an arms race, what stood out to me was the opposite: there are simply too many problems to solve and too much to discover for this to be anything but a shared effort.

I found myself particularly focused on data and application level work, especially research around recall with fidelity. Systems that can reason without hallucination, grounded in provable or verifiable inference, feel essential if AI is going to be genuinely useful and trustworthy. NeurIPS reinforced my excitement for where this field is heading and how much there still is to learn.

And as always, any trip that includes <a href="https://www.in-n-out.com/" target="_blank">my all time favorite high end cuisine in the world</a>, is a good one.
