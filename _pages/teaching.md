I love asking difficult questions : the kind that challenge inefficiency, reimagine systems, and uncover what lies beneath the surface of “how things work.” With a background in economics, computer science, and data infrastructure, I’ve worked across startups, research labs, and policy spaces to study how technology, regulation, and human behavior intersect.

My most meaningful insights don't come from code, coursework or media consumed. They come from seeing how complexity in global systems from compliance to governance often hides preventable failures of transparency and trust. 

I’m drawn to the intersections where disciplines collide where a financial model meets a blockchain ledger, or where a neural network can uncover patterns that policy overlooks. My interests span decentralized systems, AI-driven governance, data infrastructure, and trade compliance in supply chain design fields that share one core belief: that technology, when used thoughtfully, can align incentives rather than exploit them. Whether it’s building transparent compliance architectures, designing scalable graph databases, or exploring ethical automation, my curiosity lives at the edge of what connects people, data, and power.

My purpose is simple: to restore accountability to technology and intelligence to regulation.
